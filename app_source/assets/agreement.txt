1. What this is. This is an educational tool. It runs Kronos, a published research AI model (Tsinghua University, accepted at AAAI 2026, released by its authors under the MIT licence), on one-hour candles from your own Dhan account. For the stock or index you choose, it reads the last 360 one-hour candles and draws 30 possible paths for the next 24 candles. It shows their average, the range from the lowest to the highest path, and how many paths ended higher or lower. It does not know news or events.

2. What the numbers mean. The "up" and "down" percentages are only the share of the model's own 30 paths that ended higher or lower. They are the model's estimate, not a prediction you should rely on. In the author's own tests, the model's up/down calls were close to a coin flip, and the real price often moved outside the range it drew.
