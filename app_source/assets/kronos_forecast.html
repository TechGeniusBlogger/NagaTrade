How Kronos works

ChatGPT predicts the next word. Kronos predicts the next candle.

It read the last 360 hourly candles (about 60 trading days), then drew the next 24, one at a time, 30 times over. That's 30 possible futures.

The orange line is their average. The shaded band is the spread from the lowest run to the highest.

Educational demo of a research AI model on past data. Not investment advice, not a recommendation. Probabilities are the model's own estimates from its runs, not a promise of what the stock will do. Past results do not guarantee future results.
